## How to Use This Folder

This roadmap gives the recommended reading order for Reconnaissance_and_OSINT — starting with the conceptual passive/active split, moving through the general OSINT toolkit, then into specific technique-by-technique notes (search engines, DNS, subdomains, internet-wide scanners, social media, email, metadata), and finishing with the note that ties raw recon output into a usable network footprint ready for Folder 3, Network_Pentesting.

**Start here:** [[Passive_vs_Active_Recon]]
**Finish here:** [[Network_Footprinting]]

2. [[OSINT_Framework_and_Tools]] — the landscape of open-source intelligence tools and how they're organized

3. [[Google_Dorking]] — advanced search operators to surface exposed data indexed by search engines

7. [[Social_Media_OSINT]] — gathering intelligence on employees and organizational structure
8. [[Email_Harvesting]] — building target lists for phishing simulations and credential-related testing
9. [[Metadata_Extraction]] — hidden information inside publicly available documents and files

## Stage 4 — Synthesis (Capstone)

10. [[Network_Footprinting]] — combining every technique above into a structured target profile ready for Folder 3
